## Data and code from these papers

- **Roy et al. 2021.** Individual lifespans, body weights and serum metabolites are in [genenetwork/bxd_gxelongevity_2020](https://github.com/genenetwork/bxd_gxelongevity_2020) (data frozen November 2018), and in GeneNetwork under BXD Published Phenotypes and BXD-NIA-Longevity Phenotypes.
- **Arends et al. 2026.** Code is in [DannyArends/UM-HET3](https://github.com/DannyArends/UM-HET3). Lifespan and body mass data for 24,296 UM-HET3 mice are in GeneNetwork (dataset HET3-ITPPublish, trait 10001), and the figures and supplementary tables are at https://files.genenetwork.org/current/umhet3_2025/.
- **de Magalhães et al. 2018.** Survival data are in its supplementary Tables S1 and S2. The SPSS scripts are in its File S2 and at http://genomics.senescence.info/software/demographic.html.
