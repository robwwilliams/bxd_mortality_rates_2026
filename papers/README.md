### Inbreeding depression and age (cited for the F1 test)

| File | Paper | License |
|---|---|---|
| *(not stored)* | Charlesworth B, Hughes KA (1996). Age-specific inbreeding depression and components of genetic variance in relation to the evolution of senescence. *PNAS* 93:6140–6145. [doi:10.1073/pnas.93.12.6140](https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.93.12.6140) (free at [PMC39203](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC39203/)) | Copyright PNAS |
| *(not stored)* | Swindell WR, Bouzat JL (2006). Inbreeding depression and male survivorship in *Drosophila*: implications for senescence theory. *Genetics* 172:317–327. [doi:10.1534/genetics.105.045740](https://doi.org/10.1534/genetics.105.045740) (free at [PMC1456160](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC1456160/)) | Copyright Genetics Society of America |
| *(not stored)* | Fox CW, Stillwell RC (2009). Environmental effects on sex differences in the genetic load for adult lifespan in a seed-feeding beetle. *Heredity* 103:62–72. [doi:10.1038/hdy.2009.31](https://doi.org/10.1038/hdy.2009.31) | Copyright Springer Nature |

- **Charlesworth and Hughes** show that under mutation accumulation, inbreeding depression in age-specific survival grows with age. Under the simplest form of antagonistic pleiotropy, it does not. Their *Drosophila* lines fit mutation accumulation.
- **Swindell and Bouzat** found that inbreeding depression in age-specific mortality grows across the lifespan in *Drosophila*: a load of 0.307 lethal equivalents per gamete, rising by 0.046 per day.
- **Fox and Stillwell** found no rise with age in a beetle, in any rearing environment.

The de Magalhães PDF is the published version with reader highlights and margin notes removed. Nothing else in it was changed.

## Data and code from these papers

- **Roy et al. 2021.** Individual lifespans, body weights and serum metabolites are in [genenetwork/bxd_gxelongevity_2020](https://github.com/genenetwork/bxd_gxelongevity_2020) (data frozen November 2018), and in GeneNetwork under BXD Published Phenotypes and BXD-NIA-Longevity Phenotypes.
- **Arends et al. 2026.** Code is in [DannyArends/UM-HET3](https://github.com/DannyArends/UM-HET3). Lifespan and body mass data for 24,296 UM-HET3 mice are in GeneNetwork (dataset HET3-ITPPublish, trait 10001), and the figures and supplementary tables are at https://files.genenetwork.org/current/umhet3_2025/.
- **de Magalhães et al. 2018.** Survival data are in its supplementary Tables S1 and S2. The SPSS scripts are in its File S2 and at http://genomics.senescence.info/software/demographic.html.
