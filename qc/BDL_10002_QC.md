# QC of BDL_10002 (natural lifespan), GeneNetwork export of 22 October 2026

The export is kept unchanged as `data/original/BDL_10002.xlsx`.

**Input.** The GeneNetwork export of trait BDL_10002 (BXD-Longevity), 3,281 rows:
- **2,594 natural deaths:** IDs starting L (2,083), F (324, BXD×BXD F1 hybrids) and N (187, high-fat diet plus 1% NAM).
- **687 harvested animals:** IDs starting H, with Value `x`.

**Method.** `qc/qc_bdl10002.py` makes every check below and writes one row per flag to `qc/BDL_10002_flags.csv`. It also compares the export with the November 2018 freeze used in Roy et al. 2021 (`genenetwork/bxd_gxelongevity_2020/data/`). There, all 1,359 natural deaths match an L animal here by ear tag and strain, and every 2018 age equals date of death minus date of birth.

## A. Likely errors: fix before analysis

**A1. Five animals are listed twice: as a natural death and as harvested.** Each pair has the same strain, ear tag, sex, diet and age:

| Natural death | Harvested | Strain | Diet | Age (d) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| L2141 | H2141 | BXD69 | CD | 157 |
| L2047 | H2047 | BXD48 | HF | 167 |
| L1292 | H1292 | C57BL/6J | CD | 705 |
| L1573 | H1573 | DBA/2J | CD | 708 |

The ages sit near the harvest points (about 5 and about 23 months). The L copies also lack the RRID and flag columns that the H copies carry. Both suggest these were harvested and that the L rows should come out of natural lifespan.

**A2. Five ear tags belong to two animals of different strains.** Tags are otherwise unique across the whole colony. The N series overlaps 603 non-N tags but collides with only these five; if it used a separate numbering, about 52 collisions would be expected.

N2878–N2880 are odd on three counts: the other 184 N animals are NAM-fed females, while these are DBA/2J males on chow.

**A3. Two animals died before an age at which they were recorded alive.**
- **L2674 (BXD16, HF):** death at 447 d in GeneNetwork, but alive at 678 d on 15 November 2018.
- **L2641 (BXD86, HF):** death at 813 d, but alive at 836 d on the same date.

**A4. One row disagrees with itself.** L0852 (BXD53, CD) has Value 663, but Age and Longevity are both 845. The 2018 record also says 845, so Value is probably wrong.

**A5. One strain is spelled two ways.** F3310 is `BXD065XBXD102F1` (capital X); the other 21 animals of that cross are `BXD065xBXD102F1`. It will be counted as a separate strain unless fixed.

**A6. Strain identifiers (RRIDs).**
- All nine BXD175 animals have the placeholder `JAX:000GET`.
- H1789, H1790 and H1794 (BXD65) carry `JAX:009677`, which most BXD65b rows use.
- L1599 (BXD65b) carries `JAX:007110`, which most BXD65 rows use.

These don't affect ages, but they matter for anyone splitting BXD65 from BXD65b.

## C. Plausible, but worth checking against colony records

**C1. Early deaths.** 66 natural deaths came before 200 d, and 15 before 100 d.
- The youngest are L2457 (BXD77, HF, 36 d), F4549 (BXD197×BXD170 F1, CD, 53 d) and L1588 (C57BL/6J male, HF, 57 d).
- The trait excludes accidental deaths. Deaths this young are unlikely to be age-related, and the 2018 freeze had none before 98 d.
- Their causes of death decide whether they belong in the trait.

**C2. Cage-mates dying on the same day.** Cage-mates are littermates with the same birth date, so the same age at death means the same day of death.
- Same-strain pairs with tags no more than 5 apart share an exact age at death 3.0% of the time (78 of 2,558 pairs), against 0.21% for pairs farther apart (34 of 16,050). That is about a 14-fold excess.
- Eight groups of three or four died on the same day:

| Strain | Diet | Age (d) | Animals |
|---|---|---|---|
| DBA/2J | HF | 547 | L1121, L1122, L1123, L1124 |
| DBA/2J | NAM | 591 | N5030, N5031, N5032, N2924 |
| BXD86 | HF | 242 | L0253, L0255, L0257 |
| BXD91 | HF | 851 | L0869, L0870, L0871 |
| BXD49 | CD | 384 | L5595, L5596, L5598 |
| BXD100 | CD | 432 | L5626, L5627, L1191 |
| C57BL/6J | CD | 535 | L1116, L1117, L1316 |
| BXD2 | CD | 336 | L3696, L3697, L4502 |

Three or four natural deaths in one cage on one day suggest a cage event, such as flooding or the euthanasia of a whole cage. The trait definition excludes those.

**C3. Outliers within strain, diet and sex.** 71 animals sit more than 3.5 robust SDs from their group median (groups of 6 or more). Most are probably biology. Two look like possible strain mix-ups at the edge of a block of tags:
- **L0101 and L0102**, labeled BXD13 (CD), died at 728 and 548 d. BXD13 on chow has a median of 200 d, and the neighboring tags L0097–L0100 are BXD12, a strain that lives longer.
- **L0481–L0484**, a whole cage of BXD12 on the high-fat diet, all lived 1,088–1,229 d. The other 12 BXD12 on that diet have a median of 574 d.

## D. Completeness

- **D1.** Value and Longevity should be identical, but they differ in 128 rows. 126 natural deaths (68 N, 58 L) have Longevity blank, harvested H0004 has it blank instead of `x`, and L0852 is item A4. All 128 are listed, with the value each should hold, in `qc/BDL_10002_value_vs_longevity.csv`.
- **D2.** RRID is blank for 491 rows of strains that have one elsewhere, and Epoch and the KM20/SR21/EW21/KM21 flags are patchy in the same way. These are mostly the newer N and L animals.
- **D3.** Three animals alive on 15 November 2018 are missing from the export: BXD199 (tag 2773), BXD16 (tag 2686) and DBA/2J-Gpnmb (tag 2591). The last may be absent on purpose.

## For the mortality-rate analysis

Before fitting:
- Drop or resolve A1–A4.
- Merge the two spellings in A5.
- Decide on C1 and C2 by cause of death.
- Choose which source to trust for the 14 animals in B.

The same-day clusters matter more for mortality rates than for mean lifespan. A cage event puts several deaths in a single interval and distorts the interval hazard that a Gompertz fit uses.
